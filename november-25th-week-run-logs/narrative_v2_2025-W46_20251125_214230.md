## Topic Stories

### Billing

Billing issues dominated the week, accounting for 49.4% of conversations. Customers expressed frustration over billing errors and subscription management difficulties, although they appreciated the support team's responsiveness. The free-tier resolution rate was notably low at 39.5%, while the paid tier achieved a perfect resolution rate. A customer expressed their frustration: ["Yesterday, 10 dollars were taken from my account, I canceled the subscription because I don't use this tool. Can I request a refund?"](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/99cd7132_0d87_4553_b1a4_f53e87069b6c/inbox/inbox/215471903666137) (originally in Polish).

### Product Question

Product-related inquiries made up 29.1% of the conversations, with customers frustrated by disappearing presentations and file losses. However, they appreciated the support that helped recover their work. The free-tier resolution rate was high at 95.3%, with the paid tier again achieving 100%. A customer noted: ["I can't upload images into my gamma board..."](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/99cd7132_0d87_4553_b1a4_f53e87069b6c/inbox/inbox/215471912469037).

### Account

Account management issues accounted for 8.7% of the conversations. Customers appreciated the ability to manage their accounts but were frustrated by email change issues and missing credits. The free-tier resolution rate was 90.3%, with the paid tier at 100%. One customer shared: ["Hello, I can't find my account, I am logging in with my email..."](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/99cd7132_0d87_4553_b1a4_f53e87069b6c/inbox/inbox/215471907293314) (originally in Spanish).

### Bug

Bugs were a concern in 4.6% of conversations, with customers frustrated by non-working links and presentation errors. The free-tier resolution rate was 76.8%, while the paid tier achieved 100%. A customer reported: ["my top navigation bar disappeared and it's not coming back"](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/99cd7132_0d87_4553_b1a4_f53e87069b6c/inbox/inbox/215471907977444).

### Workspace

Workspace issues, including domain verification and sharing limitations, accounted for 4.1% of conversations. Users appreciated the ability to recover lost work. The free-tier resolution rate was 93.4%, with the paid tier at 100%. A user expressed: ["Hello. I purchased a domain through Gamma for my site..."](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/99cd7132_0d87_4553_b1a4_f53e87069b6c/inbox/inbox/215471858503554).

### Unknown

1.4% of conversations were categorized as unknown, with customers frustrated by the difficulty in reaching a human representative. The free-tier resolution rate was low at 31.1%, while the paid tier was at 100%. A customer stated: ["Hey, I would like to talk to a human being. Thank you."](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/99cd7132_0d87_4553_b1a4_f53e87069b6c/inbox/inbox/215471917989717).

### Privacy

Privacy concerns were raised in 0.9% of conversations. Customers appreciated security measures but were frustrated by managing privacy settings. The free-tier resolution rate was 76.6%, with the paid tier at 100%. A customer asked: ["How to make the information used here confidential..."](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/99cd7132_0d87_4553_b1a4_f53e87069b6c/inbox/inbox/215471889675226) (originally in Spanish).

### Promotions

Promotions accounted for 0.7% of conversations, with customers intrigued by offers but frustrated by unclear trial terms. The free-tier resolution rate was 90%, with the paid tier at 100%. A customer inquired: ["Hello! Are you offering any cyber monday or Black Friday sale by any chance?"](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/99cd7132_0d87_4553_b1a4_f53e87069b6c/inbox/inbox/215471941920511).

## Prioritized Actions

1. Address billing errors and improve subscription management processes.
2. Enhance data recovery processes to prevent file losses and improve customer satisfaction.
3. Improve communication channels to facilitate easier access to human representatives.
