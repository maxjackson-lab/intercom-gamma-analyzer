Billing issues dominated the discussions, reflecting customer frustration with unexpected charges and refund processes. Technical issues, particularly API errors and export failures, also emerged as key concerns, despite the product's appreciated capabilities.

## Topic Stories

### Billing
Billing issues accounted for 49.1% of the week's conversations, with customers expressing frustration over unexpected charges and refund difficulties. The free tier's resolution rate was notably low at 39%, while the paid tier achieved a perfect resolution rate. ["I meant to cancel my subscription, just got billed today"](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/99cd7132_0d87_4553_b1a4_f53e87069b6c/inbox/inbox/215471912077309) highlights the need for clearer billing communication.

### Product Question
Product-related inquiries made up 29.5% of interactions, with customers appreciating the product's capabilities but facing technical issues like API errors. The free tier resolved 95.3% of these issues, while the paid tier maintained a 100% resolution rate. ["I can't upload images into my gamma board"](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/99cd7132_0d87_4553_b1a4_f53e87069b6c/inbox/inbox/215471912469037) illustrates ongoing technical frustrations.

### Account
Account management concerns were raised in 8.9% of conversations, with customers valuing flexibility but experiencing delays in support response. The free tier's resolution rate was 88.7%, compared to 100% for the paid tier. ["Hello, I do not have access to my professional space even though the payment has been made"](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/99cd7132_0d87_4553_b1a4_f53e87069b6c/inbox/inbox/215471905638090) (originally in French) underscores access issues.

## Prioritized Actions

1. Enhance billing communication and streamline refund processes to address customer frustrations.
2. Address persistent technical issues, focusing on API errors and export failures, to improve customer satisfaction.
3. Improve support response times for account-related inquiries to enhance customer experience.
